Knowledge Base — Master Feature Catalog (complete)

This is the definitive Domainify Knowledge Base / Help Center product catalog.
Update status in place only (done / partial / blank). Do not split into parallel lists.
Ticket product details live in ticket-features.txt; this file owns KB depth and lists ticket touchpoints briefly.

Product assumption: KB is a first-class self-service product (not ticket-only) for Help Center, ticket deflection, agent reference, and in-product contextual help. UI and content locales: en / fa / ar / tr (RTL where needed). Domainify product areas (Domains, Marketplace, SMS, Wallet, Tickets, Account) are first-class content dimensions.
